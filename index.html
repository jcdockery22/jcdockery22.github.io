ORACLE

A private AI assistant that helps its developer keep track of appointments, notes, follow-ups and everyday tasks, from a computer or a phone.

What Oracle does

Calendar & appointments

Reads the developer's Google Calendar and books, moves or cancels appointments when asked.

Notes & follow-ups

Saves notes and reminders so nothing important slips through the cracks.

Morning brief

Summarizes the day ahead: upcoming appointments and open follow-ups.

Phone access

Takes requests and sends alerts through a private messaging bot that only the developer can use.

Voice

Lets the developer talk to Oracle and hear spoken replies.

Email drafts

When enabled, helps write and organize email drafts for the developer to review before sending.

Who can use it

Oracle is a personal project. It runs on the developer's own computer and is used only by the developer and their household, signed in with the developer's own accounts. There are no public sign-ups, and Oracle is not sold or offered to anyone else.

When Oracle connects to Google, it asks for access only to the developer's own Google account, and only to the parts it needs to do the jobs listed above.

Privacy

Oracle doesn't sell data, show ads, or share Google data with anyone except as described in the privacy policy. Read the full Privacy Policy to see exactly what Oracle accesses, where it's stored, and how to remove access.
