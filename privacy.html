Privacy Policy

Effective November 21, 2026

In short: Oracle is a personal assistant that runs on its developer's own computer and is used only by the developer and their household. It accesses the developer's own Google data only to provide its features. It doesn't sell data, show ads, or use Google data to train AI models.

1. About Oracle

Oracle ("the app") is a private, personal AI assistant built and operated by an individual developer for their own use. It is not a commercial product and is not offered to the public. In this policy, "you" means the person signed in to Oracle, who is the developer or a member of their household using the developer's accounts.

2. Information Oracle accesses

With your permission through Google's sign-in screen, Oracle may access:

  • Google Calendar: your calendars and events (titles, times, locations, descriptions and attendees), so Oracle can show your schedule and create, update or delete appointments when you ask.
  • Gmail (only if enabled): messages and drafts needed to help write, organize and prepare email for you to review. Oracle does not send email without your approval.

Oracle also stores information you give it directly, such as notes, reminders, follow-ups and appointment details.

3. How Oracle uses information

Oracle uses this information only to provide the features you use, such as showing and managing your schedule, preparing your morning brief, keeping track of follow-ups, and answering your requests. Oracle does not use your information for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to these features.

4. Where information is stored

Oracle runs on the developer's own computer. Notes, settings, and the sign-in tokens Google issues to Oracle are stored locally on that computer. They are not stored in an Oracle-run cloud database or shared publicly. The computer is protected by the developer's own sign-in.

5. Services Oracle uses

To work, Oracle sends information to a small number of service providers, only as needed for the feature you're using:

  • Google, to read and update your Google Calendar (and Gmail, if enabled) through Google's official APIs.
  • Anthropic (Claude API), which processes the text of your requests, and the relevant details Oracle includes with them, to understand what you asked and produce a response.
  • Telegram, when you message Oracle from your phone. Messages to and from the private Oracle bot pass through Telegram's service.

Oracle does not sell, rent or trade your information, and does not share it with advertisers or data brokers.

6. Google API Services User Data Policy

Oracle's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Specifically:

  • Google user data is used only to provide or improve Oracle's user-facing features.
  • Google user data is not transferred to others except as needed to provide those features (as described in section 5), to comply with the law, or as part of a merger or acquisition.
  • Google user data is not used for advertising, and is not sold.
  • Google user data is not used to develop, improve or train generalized AI or machine-learning models.
  • No person reads your Google data except with your permission, for security purposes, or to comply with the law.

7. Keeping or deleting your information

Oracle keeps information only as long as it's needed for its features. You can delete Oracle's local notes and data from the computer at any time. You can remove Oracle's access to your Google account at any time from your Google Account's third-party access page. Once access is removed, Oracle can no longer reach your Google data.

8. Security

Oracle connects to Google, Anthropic and Telegram over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. Sign-in tokens and API keys are kept on the developer's computer and are never published. No system is perfectly secure, but reasonable steps are taken to protect your information.

9. Children

Oracle is not offered to the public and is not directed at children.

10. Changes to this policy

If Oracle's features or data use change, this page will be updated and the effective date above will change.
